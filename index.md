## Who decides why your data is used

- For education records, your school, college, or university decides the purpose. We process those records on the Institution’s instructions.
- We decide the purpose for your app account, sign-in, security, support, and any optional use you turn on (notifications, analytics, marketing, or AI study tools).

## Consent and withdrawal

Before OTP sign-in, the app asks you to accept account creation and to turn optional purposes on or off. Change or withdraw a choice under **Settings → Privacy choices**. Withdrawal uses the same screen as giving consent. Erasing the account is **Settings → Delete my account**.

## Children

A person under 18 is a child under the DPDP Act. Students under 13 may use the app because their institution provides access. The app is an education tool, not a social network for children. Parent or guardian consent is recorded before a child’s data is processed. We do not show interest-based ads and we do not sell personal data.

## Sharing

We share data with your Institution and with Google Firebase, Cloudflare R2, Fast2SMS, Razorpay, Getepay, PayU, Paytm, Google Gemini, and OpenStreetMap when the matching feature is used. The application database is in AWS Mumbai (`ap-south-1`). Some vendors may process data outside India. We do not sell personal data.

## Security and breaches

We use access control, HTTPS to production servers, encrypted database connections, and logs. If a personal data breach occurs, we tell affected users without delay and inform the Data Protection Board of India without delay, with the further report the rules require within 72 hours.

## Retention

Account data is kept until erasure or until the purpose is over. Education and fee records are kept for the period campus or tax law requires. Attendance coordinates and face data are removed on erasure. Security logs are kept for one year unless another law requires longer.

## Your rights

- **Access:** Settings → Privacy choices → Download my data
- **Correction:** My Profile, or your Institution for an education record
- **Erasure:** Settings → Delete my account
- **Nomination:** Settings → Privacy choices → Nominee
- **Grievance:** Help & Support, or the contact below
- **Board:** you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied

## Delete your account

1. In the app: **Settings → Delete my account**.
2. Without the app: email support@atruebrand.com with the subject “Delete my AtrueCampus account” and your registered mobile number.

Web link for Google Play: https://atruecampus.github.io/atruecampus-privacypolicy.github.io/#delete-account

We complete the request within 30 days. Fee amounts may be kept where tax law requires them, with the name and address removed. Optional uses can be turned off under **Settings → Privacy choices** without deleting the account.
