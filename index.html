3) Consent and withdrawal

Before OTP sign-in, the app asks you to accept account creation and to turn optional purposes on or off. That choice is stored with the notice version and the time you made it.

You can change or withdraw a choice in the app under Settings → Privacy choices. Withdrawal is the same screen and the same number of steps as giving consent. Withdrawal does not undo processing that was already lawful. Some features stop working when you withdraw the purpose they need. Withdrawing the account purpose is done with Settings → Delete my account, which sends an erasure request.

4) Children

The Platform is used by schools as well as colleges. Students may be under 18, and some may be under 13, because the Institution gives them access. AtrueCampus is an institutional education tool. It is not a social network or a game directed at children.

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, a person under 18 is a child. Before a child’s personal data is processed, the parent or guardian’s consent must be recorded. Face attendance, precise location, health records, and marketing are used for a child only for the Institution’s education or safety purpose, with that consent on record.

For Google Play’s Families and User Data policies: we do not show third-party or interest-based advertisements, we do not sell personal data, and we do not use the Advertising ID to build an advertising profile of a child. Parents can ask the Institution, or email the contact below, to access or delete a child’s app data.

5) Who we share data with

  • Your Institution and users it authorises, for campus work.
  • Google Firebase (authentication, cloud messaging, storage, and analytics if you opt in). Phone number and sign-in tokens are processed by Firebase, which may process data outside India.
  • Cloudflare R2 for files you or the Institution upload.
  • Fast2SMS for SMS the Institution sends.
  • Razorpay, Getepay, PayU, and Paytm when a fee is paid.
  • Google Gemini when an authorised user runs the study-aid tool.
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers when the bus map is opened.
  • Authorities when the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data. The live database for the Platform is in AWS ap-south-1 (Mumbai). Vendors above may process data outside India. Transfer outside India is allowed unless the Central Government restricts a destination.

6) Security

Data sent to our production servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). The database connection uses TLS. We also use access control, expiring file links, and logs that support investigation of unauthorised access. No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will tell affected users without delay, in plain language, what happened, the likely effect, what we are doing, what you can do, and who to contact. We will also inform the Data Protection Board of India without delay and send the further report the rules require within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, unless the Board allows more time.

7) How long we keep data

  • Account and profile data: until you erase the account, or until the purpose is over.
  • Education and fee records: for the period the Institution’s rules or tax and university law require. On erasure we remove name, contact, address, health, religion, caste, photos, and identity files from the profile we control, and we keep fee rows only to the extent finance law requires.
  • Attendance coordinates and face data: removed when you erase the account.
  • Marketing contacts: not used after you withdraw marketing consent.
  • Security logs: one year, then deleted, unless another law requires longer.

8) Your rights

  • Access: Settings → Privacy choices → Download my data.
  • Correction: My Profile, or your Institution’s office for an education record.
  • Erasure: Settings → Delete my account.
  • Nomination: Settings → Privacy choices → Nominee. You may name someone to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to do so.
  • Grievance: Help & Support in the app, or the contact below. We will answer within the time the law requires. Education-record requests may also have to be completed by your Institution.
  • Complaint to the Board: if you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

11) Delete your account

Use either path. Both request deletion of the AtrueCampus account tied to your mobile number.

  1. In the app: Settings → Delete my account. Confirm the request. We then sign you out.
  2. Without the app: email support@atruebrand.com with the subject “Delete my AtrueCampus account” and the registered mobile number. This is the web link for the Google Play account-deletion field: https://atruecampus.github.io/atruecampus-privacypolicy.github.io/#delete-account.

We verify the number and complete the request without undue delay, and within 30 days. We delete or anonymise the profile, photos, identity files, face attendance, location points, and optional consents we control. Fee rows may keep the amount and a transaction reference where tax or audit law requires it, with the name and address removed. Your Institution may have to delete its own education record; we will pass that request to the Institution. You can also turn off optional uses under Settings → Privacy choices without deleting the account.

12) Permissions

The Android app may request camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, and notifications. Each request is for the feature in section 9. Denying a permission stops that feature. It does not block sign-in. The Advertising ID permission is declared for Firebase Analytics, not for showing ads.

13) Laws that apply

  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025. The main duties start on 13 May 2027. This notice is written to meet them.
  • Information Technology Act, 2000, and the SPDI Rules, 2011, for sensitive personal data such as health information, financial information, and biometric or face data, until those rules are repealed.
  • Aadhaar Act, 2016. We do not perform Aadhaar authentication with UIDAI. If an Institution uploads an Aadhaar image as an identity document, it is stored as a file for that identity check and is not published.
  • Google Play Developer Program Policies, including the User Data, Permissions, Families, and Payments policies. Campus fees are charges for real-world education services. Where Play requires its billing system for a digital product, that product uses Google Play billing.
